About Our Department

Welcome to the Department of Music & Arts at Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge and Technologies. Our mission is to foster creativity, preserve traditional art forms, and provide a comprehensive education in both Indian classical music and contemporary arts.

With highly experienced faculty, state-of-the-art practice rooms, and a thriving community of over 350+ students, we ensure an environment that nurtures artistic talent.

Our Vision

To become a premier center of excellence in arts and music, passing down cultural heritage to the next generation while embracing global artistic expressions.
